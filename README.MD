# 📊 LEO MODA | Business Intelligence Dashboard
**El-Sharnouby Group Challenge**

An interactive and professional Business Intelligence dashboard designed to analyze sales, inventory, production, marketing, and customer service data for **LEO MODA**.

---

## 🚀 Dashboard Modules & Features
* **Executive Overview:** High-level metrics on gross sales, net profit, and total revenue.
* **Inventory & Replenishment:** Track inventory levels and identify stockout priorities.
* **Customer Intelligence:** Analyze customer behavior, demographics, and top spenders.
* **Factory & Production:** Monitor production line efficiency and machine downtime reasons.
* **Marketing Performance:** Measure Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) across digital platforms.
* **Branch Intelligence:** Regional demand analysis for store expansion (e.g., Cairo vs. Alexandria).
* **Procurement & Suppliers:** Track purchase orders and supplier lead time delays.
* **Size & Fit Intelligence:** Analyze customer tickets and fit issues to minimize returns.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Python**
* **Streamlit** (Interactive SaaS Interface)
* **Plotly** (Interactive Visualizations)
* **Pandas** (Data Manipulation)
